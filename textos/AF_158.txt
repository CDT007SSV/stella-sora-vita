a15812.t | Dame Laru's Dream
a15812.c | This ... must be a dream, right?\n\nLaru looked down at her hands. They had no fingers, just smooth wooden shapes like a doll's. Her dress was stiff, her hair a solid carved block.\nOh, and she couldn't even speak!\n\nShe turned her rigid neck. She was basically a wooden toy now. Her joints made a rough clicking sound whenever she moved, like stop-motion animation that kept dropping frames.\n"Dame, do you have any other orders? Our queen will be arriving soon."\nLaru stared in confusion. She tried to ask what was going on, but she still couldn't make a sound. She could only watch as the puppet in front of her tilted its head. Like an NPC whose script had just been triggered, the puppet dutifully launched into an explanation of the game rules.\n"This is the Kingdom of Fries. The hateful Shrubshrews have taken over our fry fields, and now, we're preparing to drive them out."\n"By the way, Dame, you have a gun on your back. You'll surely be stronger than us footmen carrying swords, so please step up to the front line."\nLaru had no way of saying no. She waved her hands in a panic, but the stiff joints moved too slowly, and the soldiers ended up pushing her to the very front.\nJust then, the ground trembled. Before Laru could react, gigantic Shrubshrews charged toward them like runaway trucks.\nHer eyes flew wide. All she wanted was to run.\nNo—no no no no...!!!\n\nMorning light spilled across the window. Laru clutched her blanket tight, her brow furrowed, mumbling under her breath:\n"Stop it... Don't chew my head... Stupid Shrubshrew... Go away..."
a15813.t | Weapon Anecdote: Caribo
a15813.c | "Cracking a whip is basically like casting a fishing rod. You're great at fishing, so you'll definitely be able to learn the whip."\n\nA month before the Snowish Festival, Albedo handed Laru a long whip, along with the order to master it as quickly as possible.\nAlbedo explained that the directive came from upper leadership and that Laru had to complete it no matter what. As for why it was so sudden, and why it had to be Laru, no one bothered to tell her.\nLaru took it in stride. After all, baffling instructions from the management were nothing new. She patted her heart, promised she'd get it done, and accepted the whip with exaggerated solemnity. Then she walked out of the office, turned a corner ... and slipped out to go fishing.\n\nBut as the days passed, Laru suddenly realized that in her rush to go fishing that day, she hadn't found out what exactly counted as "knowing how to use" the whip.\n"I mean... I could just use it for fishing, right? If I tie on a float and a hook, it's a little short for regular casting... But it'd be perfect for catching fish in deep wells!"\nLaru held the whip and started trying to guess what the real purpose of the assignment had been.\nMaybe the next Monolith expedition needed someone who could fight with a whip? But the deadline was the Snowish Festival—did they want a performance?\nAfter thinking it over, her well-honed workplace instincts pointed her to the optimal answer: the whip had to work as a weapon while also looking good for show.\nShe's got it!\n\nBut as the Snowish Festival approached, nothing else was mentioned about the whip. Laru went to check with Albedo, who clearly had forgotten the whole thing.\n"Oh, right, I forgot to tell you. Some Senator saw a Snowish Festival-like event in a storybook from the Monolith and wanted to copy it. The idea was to have a few beasts pull a cart and have staff act as coachmen delivering gifts. But they later decided it was way too inefficient, so the plan got dropped."\nLaru took three seconds to process this. Then she stared at Albedo blankly. "... Huh?"\n"Anyway, the whip they gave you also came from the Monolith. You can keep it."\n\nOh, well.\nLaru told herself.\nEvery employee runs into assignments that appear out of nowhere and disappear just as mysteriously.

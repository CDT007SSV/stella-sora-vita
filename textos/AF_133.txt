a13313.t | Weapon Anecdote: Timeless Grace
a13313.c | After careful thought and consideration, Nazuka chose the staff as her weapon.\n\nOf course, she had a natural gift for large-scale magic, but that was only the foundation. If she wanted, she could have chosen a sword, a bow, or a spear instead. She simply picked the one weapon that best suited her way of fighting.\nThe windmill head design of her staff was a beautiful coincidence—it reminded her of the past. She never had any particular hobbies; her mind was always on how to improve the lives of Trekkers. She was an idealist who always looked forward.\n\nEven so, there was one thing that truly fascinated her: she loved to watch the endless turning of windmills and waterwheels. In her hometown Philae, the City of Water, that sight was everywhere, and to her, it was the most beautiful of all.
